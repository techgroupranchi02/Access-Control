# ADR 0005: Badge & pass templates

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Area:** Audience addon: registration, badges, printing
- **Relation:** Builds on [ADR 0001](0001-qr-credential-uuid-and-festival-serial.md) (badge credentials and lost badges).

## Context
Before this change there were two hard-coded print layouts:
- **Bulk QR › Print Sticker Sheets:** one fixed sticker (3 per row on A4).
- **Audience › pass dialog:** printed the on-screen dialog, which had festival text in the code ("KASHISH x BENGALURU 2026", the NGMA venue, "Nov 20–22, 2026") and invented fallbacks such as "Bangalore Attendee" and "Full-Festival Pass".

Festivals need their own designs, often several per edition (Delegate, Press, Jury, a plain QR sticker), and three kinds of print:
- **Pass + QR:** the full pass with the QR printed on it.
- **Pass only:** the pass without a QR, with a marked space for a pre-printed QR sticker.
- **QR only:** QR stickers.

## Decision
1. **Templates are festival configuration.**
   - They are designed in **Settings › Badges & Passes** (`/settings/badges`), which needs `settings.manage`.
   - Desk staff only choose a template, in the shared print dialog. The dialog is opened from Bulk QR, the Audience list ("Print passes"), the Audience pass dialog and the Assign QR success screen. Listing templates needs `registration.view`.
2. **Two ways to design.** The user asked for both:
   - `layout`: a built-in layout set by a form. It covers colours, an optional background image, the festival logo, which details show and in what order, and the QR position and size.
   - `artwork`: a finished PNG/JPG from the festival's designer, with boxes (slots) dragged onto it for the QR, name, badge no. and other details. The positions are stored in mm.
   - Every template also carries **QR sticker** settings, used for QR-only printing.
3. **One renderer** (`frontend/src/core/utils/passRenderer.js`).
   - It produces the live preview, the print dialog preview and the printed page, so what staff see is what prints.
   - Sizes are in mm.
   - Paper is either **Card printer** (one pass per page, using named `@page` sizes, so mixed template sizes work in one job) or **A4 sheet** (as many as fit, with cut lines).
   - All values are escaped.
   - Empty values are left out, never invented.
4. **Choosing a template for a delegate:**
   - first, a template linked to the delegate's category (`category_codes`);
   - then the festival default;
   - then the first saved template;
   - then a built-in **starter**.
   - Staff can also force one template for the whole job.
5. **Starters are generated in code, not stored.**
   - "Delegate pass" (A6) and "QR sticker", which reproduces the old Bulk QR sticker.
   - Every festival can print from day one, and the classic sticker is always available.
   - "Use as starting point" saves an editable copy.
6. **Images are stored in the database** as a data URL in `pass_templates.background_image` (MEDIUMTEXT, PNG/JPEG, at most 2 MB). They are never included in list responses.
   - The backend has no upload or static-file setup, and storing in the database needs no new package, folder, backup step or nginx change.
   - The browser first scales images down to 300 dpi for the pass size and re-encodes large ones as JPEG, so a save request stays under the default 1 MB nginx limit on dev.
   - Moving to file storage later would not change the API.
7. **Festival details on a pass come from the festival's own records:**
   - name from `events`;
   - logo from `film_festivals`;
   - venue, dates and hours from the saved registration form's `venue_info`, falling back to `film_festivals`.
   - A template can override the venue or dates text.
8. **Lost badges are never printed** (ADR 0001, Amendment 2): Bulk QR filters them out, and an attendee's current QR is cleared when a badge is marked lost.

## Consequences
- **New table:** `pass_templates` (migration `018_pass_templates.js`). It is our own table; no shared Freecomers table changed.
- **New API:** `/api/pass-templates`:
  - `GET /` (list and festival context), `GET /:id`;
  - `POST /`, `PUT /:id`, `POST /:id/duplicate`, `PUT /:id/default`;
  - `DELETE /:id` (archive).
- **Removed:**
  - the old inline sticker template in `BulkQrManagementPage.jsx`;
  - the iframe print of the Audience pass dialog.
- **Remaining hard-coded text (not on passes):**
  - the `KB26-` delegate label prefix, generated by the backend when a delegate registers;
  - Kashish wording in the registration form's fallback FAQ.

  Both are follow-ups.
- **PDF designs aren't uploaded directly:** they must be exported as PNG first, because browser-side PDF rendering would need an extra library.

## Amendment 1: Elements for both design types (2026-11-11)

**Context.** Artwork templates placed "boxes" from a long fixed list (QR, logo, festival name, title, holder name, category, pass type, dates, venue, serial, badge no., sequence no., every form field), and the built-in layout was an on/off list of the same fields in a fixed flow. Neither could hold mixed text ("Welcome, Ruchi"), static text, extra images or a checklist. The user asked for generic elements with **Type**, **Name**, **Position (JSON)** and **Metadata (JSON)**, stored in their own table, with 7 fonts, and the same model for the built-in layout.

**Decision**
1. **Every template is a background plus elements**, edited in one editor for both design types:
   - **Your artwork:** the background is the uploaded design.
   - **Built-in layout:** a colour background (optional image). FestiPlus places the elements (`buildLayoutElements()` in `passTemplates.service.js`), and they are then edited like any other element. "Rearrange for me" regenerates the arrangement.
2. **Three element types** in `pass_template_elements` (`type`, `name`, `position` JSON `{x, y, w, h, rotation}` in mm, `metadata` JSON, `sort_order` = stacking order):
   - **text:**
     - content with merge fields: `{{name}}`, `{{festival_name}}`, `{{category}}`, `{{pass_type}}`, `{{valid_dates}}`, `{{venue}}`, `{{serial}}`, `{{badge_no}}`, `{{sequence}}`, `{{custom.<form field>}}`;
     - font, size, weight, italic, capitals, letter spacing, alignment;
     - box fill, corners, padding;
     - "shrink to fit" and "hide when its fields are empty".
   - **image:** `source` is `upload` (stored in `pass_template_assets`), `festival_logo` or `badge_qr`. The QR and the logo are images, not types of their own. In "Pass only" printing, a Badge QR image becomes an empty box for a QR sticker.
   - **checklist:**
     - items from a multi-choice registration field, with the delegate's answers ticked (for example `pass_days`), or a typed list printed as empty boxes;
     - columns, box style, font and colour.
3. **Colours** accept `#hex`, `accent` (the template accent, or the category colour when "use each category's colour" is on) or `category`. The category ribbon is a text element with a category-colour fill, and the accent band is an empty text element with an accent fill, so no shape type is needed.
4. **7 fonts:** Inter (default), Poppins, Montserrat, Oswald, Playfair Display, Roboto Slab and Dancing Script.
   - They come from Google Fonts (already allowed by the CSP) and are loaded only where passes are shown.
   - `printInPage` runs a prepare step that lays the print area out off-screen, loads the fonts in use and fits "shrink to fit" text (`fitWhenReady()` in `passRenderer.js`) before printing.
5. **Uploaded element images** go to `pass_template_assets` through `POST /api/pass-templates/assets`, one per request, limited to 600 KB after browser compression.
   - The template save stays small, under dev's 1 MB request limit.
   - Assets belong to the event (duplicates share them).
   - Unused assets older than a day are deleted on save.
6. **Changing the pass size scales the elements:** positions by width and height, text by the smaller ratio.
7. **Migration `021_pass_template_elements.js`** converted existing templates:
   - artwork slots became elements in the same positions;
   - built-in field lists became the generated arrangement;
   - `config_json` now holds only `background`, `accent`, `useCategoryColor` and `sticker`. The QR-only sticker is unchanged.

**Consequences**
- **Removed:** the old built-in "Details on the pass" list and QR position/size controls, and `config.slots`, `config.fields` and `config.qr`.
- **Starters:** the starter templates are generated as element lists.
- **One renderer path:** `renderElements` serves both design types.

## Amendment 2: QR Settings in the print dialog and A4 page layout (2026-11-11)

**Context.** QR-only sticker settings were stored per template, yet QR-only printing only used the template for those settings. Staff also had no control over how many passes or stickers go on an A4 page.

**Decision**
1. **QR Settings are festival-wide.**
   - They cover sticker width, the line under the festival name, and whether the festival name, badge no. and sequence no. show.
   - They are stored in `pass_print_settings` (one row per event; migration `022_pass_print_settings.js` copied each festival's values from its default template and removed `sticker` from template configs).
   - They are set in the print dialog when **QR only** is chosen:
     - changes apply to that print;
     - users with `settings.manage` can **Save as festival default** (`PUT /api/pass-templates/qr-settings`);
     - the list response carries `qr_settings` and `can_manage`.
   - **Why not `audience_settings`:** the Audience settings page saves its whole JSON at once and would overwrite these values.
2. **No Template picker for QR only.** The template editor no longer has a sticker card.
3. **A4 layout:** orientation (portrait or landscape) and **columns × rows**, each from 1 up to the most that fit at true size.
   - Spacing: 8 mm margins and cut gaps of at least 4 mm (`sheetLayout()` in `passRenderer.js`).
   - Pieces are never scaled; fewer per page only adds space.
   - A job with mixed template sizes uses a grid based on its largest piece.
   - The choice is remembered per viewer and mode.
   - The preview shows the first A4 page with the real pieces, plus "Page 1 of N".
   - The card printer is unchanged (one piece per page at its own size).
4. The starter "QR sticker" is renamed **Small QR pass**, since it is now an ordinary 58 × 62 mm pass template.
5. **Grids beyond true size (2026-11-11).** Columns × rows can go up to 6 × 8 for any piece. Options that fit at true size are marked ✓. When the chosen grid doesn't fit, staff choose one of:
   - **Shrink to fit:** every piece is scaled by the same factor, shown as a percentage.
   - **Margins: None:** 0 mm margin and gap, at 100%, with a warning that office printers may clip the outer 3–5 mm.

   Until one is chosen, Print stays disabled. For example, four 105 × 145.55 mm passes on A4 print at 91% with standard margins, or at 100% with no margins.
6. **A4 only (2026-11-11).** The print dialog no longer offers the card printer: every print is an A4 sheet. Margins are a single "No margins" switch (off = 8 mm margin, 4 mm cut gaps). The card-printer code path in `renderSheet` is kept but unused.
7. **Margins switch (2026-11-11).** A compact "No margins" switch sits under the orientation control, with the printer-edge warning in its ⓘ tooltip. It is off by default (standard margins); a viewer's saved choice wins.
8. **Rotation in 45° steps (2026-11-11).** Elements can turn 0–315° in 45° steps. At 90° and 270° the content turns inside its box, so the box stays the area it fills; at the other 45° steps the whole box turns around its centre. The properties panel also has line height (text) and Centre on the pass (horizontally, vertically or both).
